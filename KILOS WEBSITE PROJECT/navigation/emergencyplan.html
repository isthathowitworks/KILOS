Hypertensive Crisis Emergency

If your blood pressure is consistently above 180/120 mmHg and you have symptoms, seek immediate medical help.

call CALL 911

Emergency Action Steps

1

Stay Calm & Sit Down

Find a comfortable chair, support your back, and keep your feet flat on the floor. Do not panic.

2

Wait 5 Minutes

Rest quietly for 5 minutes without talking, moving, or looking at your phone.

3

Retest Blood Pressure

Check your BP again. If it is still over 180/120, proceed to the next step immediately.

4

Call for Help

Call your family member or emergency contacts. Describe your symptoms clearly.

info

Symptoms to watch out for: Chest pain, shortness of breath, back pain, numbness/weakness, change in vision, or difficulty speaking.
