What is Hypertension?

Hypertension, or high blood pressure, happens when the force of blood pushing against your artery walls is consistently too high. Over time, this can damage your heart and blood vessels.

visibility

Why Monitor Regularly?

High blood pressure often has no symptoms. Regular checking is the only way to know if your levels are safe. Monitoring at home helps your doctor make better decisions about your care.

Blood Pressure Categories

Use this guide to understand your numbers. The top number is Systolic, and the bottom is Diastolic.

Category Systolic (Top) Diastolic (Bottom)
Normal Less than 120 and Less than 80
Elevated 120 – 129 and Less than 80
High (Stage 1) 130 – 139 or 80 – 89
High (Stage 2) 140 or higher or 90 or higher
Hypertensive Crisis Higher than 180 and/or Higher than 120

* Always consult your healthcare provider to understand your specific target range.
